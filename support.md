# Vibely — Support

Questions, problems, safety concerns, or data requests — email
**[spacevibely@gmail.com](mailto:spacevibely@gmail.com)**. We aim to reply within 2 business
days, and to **acknowledge a formal grievance within 24 hours** — see
[Grievances](#privacy-requests-and-grievances) below.

---

## Reporting someone or something

Every event, message, and user in Vibely can be reported from inside the app. Open the item,
choose **Report**, and pick a reason. You can block the user at the same time from the same
screen.

**We review every report and act on objectionable content within 24 hours**, removing content
and removing the user where warranted. Every decision is made by a person, not an algorithm.
See the [Terms of Service]({{ '/terms-of-service/' | relative_url }}) §4.

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

## Safety when meeting people

Vibely helps you find events; it does not organise, vet, or supervise them, and we do not
verify the identity of any user. When you attend an event you are meeting people you may not
know.

- Meet in public places, especially the first time.
- Tell someone you trust where you are going and when you expect to be back.
- Arrange your own transport and keep the means to leave.
- Do not share your home address, financial details, or identity documents.
- Leave, and report the user, if anything feels wrong.

## Common questions

**I can't sign in.**
Vibely uses Google and Apple sign-in only — there is no password to reset. If sign-in fails,
check you are online, then try the other provider. Still stuck? Email us with the address you
signed up with.

**I'm not getting notifications.**
Check notifications are allowed for Vibely in your device settings. Notifications cover event
updates, chat messages, and friend requests.

**The map is blank or won't centre on me.**
Vibely needs location permission to show events near you. It is only read while the app is
open — never in the background. You can still browse by panning and searching manually if you
prefer to leave it off.

**How do I delete my account?**
Profile → **Delete account**. It is permanent, not a deactivation. Full details, including how
to request deletion if you have already uninstalled, are on the
[Delete Your Account]({{ '/account-deletion/' | relative_url }}) page.

**Who can see my details?**
Other users see your name or username and whatever profile details you chose to fill in, plus
events you host and messages you send in group chats. Your email address is never shown to
other users. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}).

**Do I need to be 18?**
Yes. Vibely is for users aged 18 and over.

**My account was removed. How do I appeal?**
Email us with "Appeal" in the subject line, from the address you signed up with. A person
reviews every appeal. See the [Terms of Service]({{ '/terms-of-service/' | relative_url }}) §4.

**Someone is using my copyrighted work.**
Email us with "Copyright complaint" in the subject line. The details we need are listed in the
[Terms of Service]({{ '/terms-of-service/' | relative_url }}) §7.
